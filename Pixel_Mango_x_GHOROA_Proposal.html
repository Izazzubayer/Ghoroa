Summary

GHOROA's digital opportunity.

GHOROA is a 40+ year heritage restaurant with a loyal customer base. Much of today's online ordering runs through third-party platforms — valuable for reach and delivery, but costly in margins and customer ownership.

We're not replacing those platforms overnight. We're building GHOROA's own digital ecosystem to:

  • Create a premium online presence
  • Enable direct customer ordering
  • Build long-term customer relationships
  • Reduce dependency on third-party platforms
  • Lay the foundation for future growth

Current Challenges

Where GHOROA is today.

Business

  • Platform commissions reduce profitability
  • No direct customer ownership
  • No customer database

Technology

  • Limited online discoverability

  • No restaurant dashboard
  • Manual order management
  • No customer analytics

Brand
