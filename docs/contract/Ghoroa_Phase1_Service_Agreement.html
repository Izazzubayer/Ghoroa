3.  What is not included

  • Customers ordering food on the website, paying online, or a kitchen / staff screen
  • Website hosting, domain name (.com), or other monthly tools — Ghoroa pays those separately
  • Food photography, or actually printing cards, menus, or packaging
  • Ongoing Facebook/Instagram posting, ads, or monthly marketing
  • Running delivery — Ghoroa’s own team handles that
  • Later extras such as loyalty cards, gift cards, table booking, or multi-branch tools
  • Anything extra — a new feature, page, or piece of work not listed in Section 2 — is not included. It will be charged separately. The price depends on how complex that extra work is. Pixel Mango will send a quote before starting it.

4.  Phase 2 is a later conversation

A second phase may come later: online ordering, a database with outside vendors, a kitchen dashboard, and payments (bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, and similar). That work is not in this agreement. No Phase 2 price or date is agreed here. It will only start after more meetings and a new signed agreement with its own fee.

5.  Timeline

Phase 1 takes 10 weeks after work starts.

Work starts only when both sides have signed and Ghoroa has paid the first 50%. Pixel Mango will not start before that payment.

  • Week 1 — Discovery. Understand the restaurant, brand, menu, photos, locations, and goals. Collect what we need from Ghoroa to begin.
  • Week 2 — Brand identity. Logo directions, colours, type, and the overall look. First round of brand feedback.
  • Week 3 — Brand lock-in. Finish the chosen logo and brand system. Business card and social profile / cover designs.
  • Week 4 — Print design. Packaging (bags, boxes, sleeves) and the restaurant menu + juice bar menu, ready for print.
  • Week 5 — Website design. Brand guidelines and the look of the website pages, so the site is built from the same system.
  • Week 6 — Website build. Build the pages in Bangla and English (Home, Menu, About, Contact, Locations, FAQ, Privacy, Terms). Maps and Google Business.
  • Week 7 — Writing and privacy. Website copy, FAQ text, and a draft privacy page for the lawyer to review.
  • Week 8 — Search and testing. Local search setup (SEO), checks on phones and computers, and remaining design changes within the 3 rounds.
  • Week 9 — Legal check and polish. Lawyer review of the privacy page. Final fixes, performance, and Ghoroa’s last approvals.
  • Week 10 — Go live and handoff. Website goes live. Brand, packaging, menu, and website files are handed over after the final 20% is paid.

If Ghoroa is slow to send feedback, photos, menu lists, or approvals, the timeline pauses. Pixel Mango is not responsible for delays from printers, Google, domain companies, or the lawyer.

6.  Money

Total for Phase 1: Tk 2,00,000 (two lakh taka).

  • Start — 50%: Tk 1,00,000 when you sign. No work starts until this is paid.
  • Midway — 30%: Tk 60,000 around week 5, when the brand, packaging, menus, and website look are ready for review. Work on the remaining weeks pauses if this is not paid.
  • Handoff — 20%: Tk 40,000 when the website goes live. Final design and website files are given after this is paid.

Pay in taka by bank transfer using the details on the invoice. Extra costs like domain, hosting, printing, or any seperate fee (beyond what is mentioned) are Ghoroa’s to pay, unless an invoice says otherwise.

Any extra or additional feature outside this agreement will be charged extra. The rate will be set by Pixel Mango based on how complex the feature is, and work on it starts only after Ghoroa accepts that quote in writing.

7.  Changes (revisions)

Each design or page includes 3 rounds of changes that stay within the original plan. Please send all comments for a round in one go.

A new idea, extra page, extra packaging item, extra menu version, or any new feature not in Section 2 is not a “change” — it is extra work. It will be charged separately. The price depends on how complex that work is. After 3 rounds, more edits to the original plan cost Tk 2,500 per extra round, unless we agree another amount in writing.

8.  What Ghoroa needs to do

  • Name one person who can approve work
  • Send menu items, prices, photos, address, Google Business access, and any existing logo files when asked
  • Reply in writing within 5 working days. If there is no reply, that round may be treated as approved
  • Pay on time. Do not ask Pixel Mango to start before the first 50% is paid
  • Help with the lawyer’s privacy check (how Ghoroa actually handles customer information)
  • Make sure you have the right to use any photos or text you give us

9.  What Pixel Mango will do

  • Do the Phase 1 work carefully and professionally
  • Keep Ghoroa updated on progress
  • Deliver what is listed in Section 2, if Ghoroa also does its part
  • Build the website from the brand guide, so it looks like one brand
  • Keep Ghoroa’s private information private (see Section 11)

10.  Who owns the work

Until the final (handoff) payment is fully paid, the work belongs to Pixel Mango.

After full payment, Ghoroa owns the custom work made for this project: logo, packaging and menu artwork, website as delivered, writing, and brand guide — for Ghoroa’s business use.

Pixel Mango keeps its own general tools and methods. Pixel Mango may show the project in its portfolio unless Ghoroa asks in writing not to. Pixel Mango does not own Ghoroa’s old logo, recipes, or photos. Fonts or plugins from other companies stay under those companies’ rules.

If Ghoroa uses the work before paying in full, Pixel Mango can ask Ghoroa to stop using it until payment is complete.

11.  Keeping things private

Both sides will keep each other’s private business information private, and use it only for this project. This lasts for 3 years after the site goes live or after this agreement ends, whichever is later. It does not cover information that is already public, or that the law requires to be shared.

Pixel Mango may say Ghoroa is a client, unless Ghoroa asks otherwise in writing.

12.  Fixes after launch

For 14 days after the website goes live, Pixel Mango will fix real bugs for free (for example: broken pages, broken links, Bangla/English switch, or maps not working).

This does not cover new features, Ghoroa changing its own text, hosting problems, or anything from Phase 2. After 14 days, extra help is charged, or covered only if we sign a separate maintenance plan. “I changed my mind about the look” after you already approved it is not a bug.

13.  Ending this agreement

Either side may end this agreement with 14 days’ written notice. If Pixel Mango seriously fails to do the work and does not fix it within 14 days of a written warning, Ghoroa may end it. Pixel Mango may end it immediately for non-payment, abusive behaviour, or illegal requests.

If it ends: Ghoroa pays for work already done, plus any outside costs already spent. Payments already made are counted toward that. If more is owed, Ghoroa pays the rest within 7 days. If Ghoroa has paid more than the work done, Pixel Mango refunds the unused amount within 14 days (minus costs that cannot be cancelled). Unpaid work is not handed over. Phase 2 does not start unless a new agreement is signed.

14.  If something goes wrong

If Pixel Mango is found responsible for a loss under this agreement, the most it would pay is the money Ghoroa has actually paid Pixel Mango for Phase 1.

Pixel Mango is not responsible for lost sales, lost data, or problems caused by how Ghoroa uses the site — except where Bangladesh law does not allow that limit.

Pixel Mango cannot promise Google rankings, more customers, or that Ghoroa will stop using FoodPanda. Phase 1 is a foundation, not a sales guarantee.

15.  Other simple rules

This document (plus the Phase 1 proposal, where it does not clash) is the full deal for Phase 1. If this document and the proposal disagree, this document wins. Small notes can be confirmed by email. Price changes need a written quote that Ghoroa accepts. If one sentence is invalid, the rest still stands. This is not a partnership or an employment contract. Messages can be sent to the emails in Section 1.
