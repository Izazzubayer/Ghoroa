WHEREAS

A.  The Client operates GHOROA, a heritage restaurant, and wishes to engage the Agency to create a Phase 1 digital foundation comprising brand identity, selected print design, a public website, content, privacy policy, SEO setup, and extensible technical architecture.

B.  The Agency has submitted a proposal dated 28 July 2026, and the Client has agreed to proceed with Phase 1 only.

C.  The Parties wish to record the terms of that engagement in writing.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1.  PURPOSE

1.1  The purpose of this Agreement is to appoint the Agency to perform the Phase 1 services described herein for the Client.

1.2  The work provided initially under this Agreement is Phase 1 — Foundation only. It does not include a live ordering system, payments integration, kitchen dashboard, or related operational software.

  • Ongoing marketing retainers, social posting, email campaigns, or SEO beyond the Phase 1 setup in Clause 3.1;

  • Later expansions such as loyalty programmes, gift cards, QR table ordering, reservations, customer accounts, multi-branch or franchise systems, and CRM;
  • Any work not listed in Clause 3.1, which shall be quoted separately.

4.  PHASE 2 (NOT COMMISSIONED)

4.1  The Parties acknowledge that a possible later phase (“Phase 2”) may include, without limitation: database setup with third-party vendors; kitchen dashboard; kitchen printing; cart and checkout; and payment integrations including bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz and related gateways, together with associated marketing (“Awareness”).

4.2  Phase 2 is expressly excluded from this Agreement. No Phase 2 price, timeline or vendor is agreed herein. Phase 2 shall be defined only upon further meetings with the Client and relevant third-party vendors, and shall require a separate written agreement and a separate fee.

5.  PROJECT TIMELINE

5.1  Phase 1 is estimated at six to eight (6–8) weeks from the Project Start Date.

5.2  The Project Start Date is the date on which (a) both Parties have signed this Agreement, and (b) Invoice 1 (fifty percent) has been received in full. Work shall not commence before payment of Invoice 1.

5.3  Indicative schedule:

  • Week 1 — Discovery, research and strategy;
  • Week 2 — Brand identity direction;
  • Weeks 3–4 — Guidelines, packaging and menus, website design and build, content, privacy policy draft;
  • Week 5 — Quality assurance, polish, and scheduled lawyer review of the privacy policy;
  • Week 6 (with buffer to Week 8) — Go-live, asset handover, remaining revisions within policy.

5.4  Delays caused by the Client (including late feedback, missing copy, photographs, menu data, access or approvals) shall pause the timeline. The Agency shall not be liable for delays of third parties (including printers, Google, domain registrars, or lawyers).

6.  PAYMENT TERMS

6.1  The total fee for Phase 1 is Taka 2,04,000 (Taka Two Lakh Four Thousand) only, exclusive of VAT or other tax if applicable by law.

6.2  The fee shall be paid as follows:

  • Invoice 1 — 50% advance: Taka 1,02,000, due upon signing. Work shall not start until this sum is received.
  • Invoice 2 — 50% on delivery: Taka 1,02,000, due on go-live / delivery of Phase 1 assets, and before final handover of source files.

6.3  Payment shall be made in Bangladeshi Taka by bank transfer, bKash or Nagad to the details stated on the invoice. Third-party costs (domain, hosting, printing, and any lawyer disbursements beyond the privacy-policy fee in Clause 3.1) are the Client’s responsibility unless an invoice states otherwise.

7.  LATE PAYMENT FEES

7.1  Save for Invoice 1 (which is payable before work starts), invoices are due within seven (7) days of issue. Sums unpaid after the due date shall attract a late fee of two percent (2%) per month or part thereof on the outstanding balance.

7.2  The Agency may suspend work, withhold files, and/or withhold publication from Agency-controlled environments until overdue amounts are cleared. Periods of suspension shall not count against the 6–8 week timeline.

8.  REVISION LIMITS

8.1  Each design or page deliverable includes three (3) rounds of reasonable revisions within the original brief. Feedback shall be consolidated in each round.

8.2  New concepts, additional pages, additional SKUs, additional packaging items, additional menu variants, or other changes of scope are not revisions and shall be quoted separately. Further edits after three rounds shall be charged at a rate agreed in writing, or Taka 2,500 per additional round if no other rate is agreed.

9.  CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

The Client shall:

  • (a) nominate a single decision-maker for approvals;
  • (b) supply menu items, prices, photographs, location details, Google Business access, domain/hosting access, and existing brand assets promptly;
  • (c) give written feedback within five (5) business days of each presentation (silence after five days may be treated as approval of that round);
  • (d) pay invoices on time and not require the Agency to commence work before Invoice 1 is paid;
  • (e) cooperate in the lawyer’s review of the privacy policy, including factual accuracy of how GHOROA handles data; and
  • (f) obtain all licences and permissions for content supplied by the Client.

10.  AGENCY RESPONSIBILITIES

The Agency shall:

  • (a) perform Phase 1 with reasonable professional skill and care;
  • (b) keep the Client reasonably informed of progress;
  • (c) deliver the items in Clause 3.1, subject to the revision policy and to the Client’s cooperation;
  • (d) use the Figma brand guidelines as the source of truth for website user interface; and
  • (e) not disclose the Client’s confidential information except as permitted by Clause 12.

11.  OWNERSHIP AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

11.1  Until Invoice 2 is paid in full, all work product remains the property of the Agency.

11.2  Upon full payment, the Agency assigns to the Client all right, title and interest in the custom Phase 1 deliverables created for GHOROA under this Agreement (including wordmark and custom graphics, packaging and menu artwork, website custom code as delivered, copy and guidelines), for the Client’s business use.

11.3  The Agency retains its pre-existing tools, frameworks, methods and generic libraries, and a non-exclusive licence to display the work in portfolios and case studies unless the Client objects in writing for a stated confidentiality period. The Agency acquires no ownership of the Client’s pre-existing marks, recipes or content. Third-party fonts, stock, plugins and software remain under their own licences, which the Client must maintain after handover.

11.4  If the Client uses unpaid work, the Agency may revoke licences and require immediate cessation of use until payment is complete.

12.  CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1  Each Party shall keep confidential the other Party’s non-public business, technical, financial and customer information, and shall use it only to perform this Agreement.

12.2  This obligation shall continue for three (3) years after the later of go-live or termination, and shall not apply to information that is public (other than by breach), independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

12.3  The Agency may name GHOROA as a client unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.

13.  WARRANTY

13.1  For fourteen (14) days after go-live, the Agency shall, at no extra charge, remedy defects in Phase 1 website functionality that materially fail to match the agreed scope (including broken layouts, links, bilingual toggle and maps).

13.2  This warranty does not cover Client content edits, hosting misconfiguration, third-party outages, new features, or any Phase 2 item. After fourteen days, support is chargeable or covered only under a separate maintenance agreement. Differences of taste after sign-off are not defects.

14.  TERMINATION

14.1  Either Party may terminate this Agreement by fourteen (14) days’ written notice. The Client may terminate for material breach if the Agency does not cure within fourteen (14) days of written notice. The Agency may terminate immediately for non-payment, abusive conduct, or illegal requests.

14.2  Upon termination, the Client shall pay for work completed up to the effective date (pro rata as reasonably documented by the Agency), plus third-party costs incurred. Invoice 1 shall be applied against that amount. If completed value exceeds sums paid, the Client shall pay the difference within seven (7) days. If sums paid exceed completed value, the Agency shall refund the unused balance within fourteen (14) days, less non-cancellable third-party costs. Unpaid custom work shall not be transferred. Phase 2 shall not proceed under a terminated Phase 1 unless a new agreement is signed.

15.  LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

15.1  The Agency’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees actually paid by the Client to the Agency under this Agreement.

15.2  The Agency shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential loss, lost profits, lost data, or third-party claims arising from the Client’s use of the site or brand, except to the extent such limitation is not permitted by the laws of Bangladesh.

15.3  The Agency does not warrant search-engine rankings, sales volume, or independence from third-party delivery platforms. Phase 1 is a foundation and not a guarantee of commercial results.

16.  DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

16.1  The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

16.2  If unresolved, the dispute shall be submitted to the courts of Dhaka, Bangladesh. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Bangladesh.

17.  GENERAL

17.1  This Agreement, together with the Phase 1 proposal (as amended hereby), constitutes the entire agreement for Phase 1 and supersedes prior oral discussions. In the event of conflict, this Agreement shall prevail.

17.2  Amendments shall be in writing. Email confirmation by both Parties may suffice for minor scope notes; changes to fees require a written quotation accepted by the Client.

17.3  If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder shall continue in force.

17.4  Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or employment relationship.

17.5  Notices may be sent to the addresses or emails set out in Clause 2.
