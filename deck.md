Pixel Mango is a Digital Product Studio.

# About GHOROA

GHOROA is a heritage Bangladeshi restaurant with over 40 years of history.

Currently most of their online business comes through FoodPanda.

The goal is to create their own digital ecosystem so customers order directly from GHOROA instead of through marketplaces.

This reduces commissions, improves customer relationships and creates long-term business value.
